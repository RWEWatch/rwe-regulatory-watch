## What this is

A weekly watch on **real-world evidence (RWE), regulatory science, health technology assessment and evidence methodology**. It monitors regulators and HTA bodies on four continents, international bodies (ICH, WHO, CIOMS, ICMRA, IMDRF), EU law (EUR-Lex), the US Federal Register, trial registries (ClinicalTrials.gov, HMA-EMA RWD catalogues) and the scientific literature (PubMed, Europe PMC, Crossref online-first articles of key journals, arXiv).

Published by **{publisher}**, an automated publication.

## AI-generated content — transparency notice

In line with **Article 50(4) of the EU Artificial Intelligence Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689)**, readers are informed that:

- **Each edition is generated by an AI system** and published **without prior human editorial review**. The text is therefore disclosed as artificially generated.
- **How it works:** a program collects new items from public sources every week. An AI model (**Google Gemini, via the free tier of the Gemini API**) scores their relevance, selects the most substantial ones and writes the summaries and analyses. The program runs on **GitHub Actions** and the archive is published with **GitHub Pages**.
- **Safeguards:** the model only receives items that were actually collected; every link in an edition is automatically checked against the collected sources and unknown links are removed; press items are flagged as secondary sources; preprints and registry entries are flagged as such; every edition lists which sources could not be reached.
- **Limits:** AI output can contain errors, omissions, misattributions or over-interpretation. **Always verify against the linked primary source** before relying on, citing or reusing any item. This watch is for information only and is **not legal, regulatory, medical or investment advice**.
