### Methodological Pitfalls in Target Trial Emulation: "Laundered Survival" and GLP-1 RA Cancer Studies
- **Date / organisation / jurisdiction:** 20 November 2026 / Journal of Clinical Epidemiology / International
- **Summary:** A conceptual and quantitative evaluation demonstrated that observational studies requiring post-baseline exposure (e.g., ≥2 prescriptions) while accruing follow-up from the first prescription induce severe immortal time bias. - **Why it matters:** Demonstrates how misaligning eligibility, treatment assignment, and follow-up start (EAT0) invalidates target trial emulation assumptions and generates artifactual treatment benefits.
